Pixel Battle

Welcome to the GCTU Tech Club open day!

One shared canvas. Four teams. Paint, claim territory and defend it, live with everyone on the Wi-Fi.

How to play

  1. Pick a tool: Pen, Line, Circle, Polygon or Bucket.
  2. Pick a colour from the palette (your team colour is in there).
  3. Tap the canvas to place points. Drag to move, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  4. Press Place to paint it for everyone to see.

Rules

  • You can paint once every 5 seconds (watch the cooldown bar).
  • Each shape is limited: circle radius up to 20, polygons up to 8 corners, bucket fills up to 1500 pixels.
  • The team with the most pixels on the canvas wins.
  • Be kind: no offensive drawings.
