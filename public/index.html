Pixel Battle

Welcome to the GCTU Tech Club open day!

One shared canvas. Four teams. Paint, claim territory and defend it, live with everyone on the Wi-Fi.

How to play

Normal Battle

  • Pick a team and drawing tool.
  • Draw inside your team's coloured zone.
  • Wait 5 seconds between actions.
  • Your team scores the pixels it owns, whatever their colour.
  • Highest score wins. A tie is a draw.

Build a House Challenge

  • Build the reference house inside your team's zone.
  • Draw its walls, roof, door, windows and sun.
  • Use Pen, Line, Circle, Polygon, Fill or DDA.
  • Follow your checklist and live marks.

Rules

  • Normal Battle: the team with the most owned pixels wins. Equal scores are a draw.
  • Build a House: earn 60 house marks and 40 algorithm marks (100 total).
  • Build a House: The host sets the challenge time. Highest marks win; equal marks are a draw.
  • Be kind: no offensive drawings.
